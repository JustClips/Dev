# UI style: Sweep the Moon

The Visual Language Card every screen follows. Layouts are generated by
`tools/gen_ui.py` into `src/gui/*.model.json`; edit the script, not the JSON.

```
GAME:            Sweep the Moon for Alien Eggs. The whole server sweeps Moon dust,
                 finds alien eggs and hatches pets that earn cash in their park.
AUDIENCE:        9-13
GENRE:           egg / pet sim (loop of Steal an Egg + Grow a Garden)
WORLD MATERIALS: white spacesuit plastic, grey moon regolith, glowing alien goo
FAMILY:          Stud Block, mutated to a night-sky moon palette
PALETTE:
  bg        #141A33   night navy: dim overlay, dark plates (Moon bar, toasts)
  surface   #E9EEF7   spacesuit white stud panels, rows
  header    #3B4FA8   blue stud header strip (shop header is gold, rebirth purple)
  ink       #1B1F3A   outlines and dark text
  accent    #7CF5C8   alien mint: Moon Clean bar, selected tab. Nothing else.
  premium   #FFC83C   Robux buttons (with the Robux glyph), offers
  danger    #FF4D5E   close X, FULL tag, prices you can't afford
  success   #3DDC5A   Buy with cash, Collect, Rebirth, Base pill
MOTIF:           Roblox stud texture tinted per panel (art/out/ui/Stud.png), 4px navy outline
CORNERS:         none. Square stud blocks everywhere (Dev: rounded corners look bad)
STROKE:          4px #1B1F3A on panels and buttons; 2.5px round-join stroke on white text
TYPE:            Montserrat only (rbxasset://fonts/families/Montserrat.json)
                 display Heavy (titles, buttons, numbers)
                 body    Bold / ExtraBold (descriptions, chips)
BUTTONS:         every button has a solid fill (tabs and zone rows are header blue);
                 never a bare label on a panel
MOTION:          pop-squash: press 0.94, hover 1.06 (PC), modal 0.85 -> 1 Back Out 0.24 s,
                 toasts pop in and fade. ReducedMotion turns all of it off.
DENSITY:         HUD-sparse (edges only; the middle of the screen is the Moon)
NEVER:           white text straight on the world (always a plate), emoji as icons,
                 more than one loud colour per screen
ALWAYS:          icon + label on HUD buttons, red X top-right on modals,
                 green = spend cash, gold = spend Robux
```

## HUD (edge-rail dialect, like Steal an Egg / Grow a Garden)

- **Left rail**: Shop (gold), Index (blue), Rebirth (purple) stud tiles with
  icon + label.
- **Top centre pills** teleport you:
  - Base (green) goes to your own spawn.
  - Sell (orange) goes to the Rocket.
  - Zones (blue) opens a picker. It shows the unlock price or "Rebirth N".
    A locked zone sends you to its gate.
- **Under the pills**: the Moon Clean bar (meteor countdown during events),
  boost chips and toasts.
- **Wallet, bottom-left**: the cash chip with a `+` (opens Shop > Cash) and the
  dust backpack meter with a FULL tag. On phones the wallet moves top-left,
  away from the thumbstick.
- **Right side**:
  - The upgrade card: next tool, what it does, and a green Buy button. It turns
    grey when you can't afford it.
  - The Drive and Auto buttons (shown only when you own those).
  - A round SWEEP button on phones, next to the jump button.

## Screens

| Screen | Purpose | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| Shop | Deals (featured Starter Pack well), Tools, Eggs, Passes, Cash | Buy (green cash / gold Robux) |
| Index | Every alien in a 3D viewport; missing ones are silhouettes with a lock | Close |
| Rebirth | Cash multiplier now -> after, cost (red if short) | Rebirth! |
| Offer | Contextual Robux offer, bottom centre, non-blocking | Buy |
| WelcomeBack | Offline income | Collect |
| Hatch | Egg shake -> flash -> alien happy dance -> flies to your park | Tap to skip |

## Art

Icons are Blender renders (`art/blender/icons.py`): chunky toy shapes with one
navy outline. Logo and banners come from ForgeUI. All of them are uploaded by
`tools/upload_assets.py` and referenced through `Config/Assets.luau`.
